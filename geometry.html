Core toolkit

Angles, similar triangles, cyclic quadrilaterals, power of a point, area methods, transformations and coordinates form a versatile toolkit.

Diagram discipline

  • Mark equal angles and lengths immediately.
  • Search for similar triangles.
  • Check whether four points may be concyclic.
  • Try reflection, rotation or an auxiliary line.
  • Never assume a picture is to scale—prove what you use.
A diagram suggests a conjecture; the proof establishes it.
