Olympiad Mathematics

What is Olympiad Mathematics?

Olympiad mathematics is a style of problem solving built around non-routine questions. Problems often look simple to state but require a clever observation, a structural argument or a carefully constructed proof.

The goal is not to know every trick. It is to become comfortable searching for the right idea.

What makes a solution strong?

  1. Understand exactly what is being asked.
  2. Experiment with small cases and patterns.
  3. Find a useful invariant, symmetry or transformation.
  4. Write a complete argument that another reader can verify.

The four pillars

Number theory, algebra, geometry and combinatorics overlap constantly. Learning connections between them is one of the most valuable skills an Olympiad student can develop.
