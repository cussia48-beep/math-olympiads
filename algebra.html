High-value techniques

Master factorization, substitutions, inequalities, polynomial identities, sequences and functional equations.

(a+b)² ≥ 4ab   for a,b ≥ 0

Look for structure

  • Normalize variables when possible.
  • Try symmetric substitutions such as x+y and xy.
  • Use AM-GM when products and sums interact.
  • Factor before applying complicated tools.
  • Check equality cases throughout an inequality proof.

Proof mindset

A beautiful algebraic solution often replaces a long computation with one well-chosen identity.
