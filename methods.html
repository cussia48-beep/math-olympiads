Methods of Approaching Olympiad Problems

A repeatable process for moving from “I have no idea” to a rigorous solution.

Olympiad Mathematics
1

Decode

Rewrite the statement in your own words. Identify variables, constraints and the exact target.

2

Explore

Try small cases, draw a diagram, factor examples or compute a few values.

3

Conjecture

Ask what pattern seems inevitable. Then actively try to disprove your guess.

4

Choose a tool

Pick the simplest fitting idea: invariant, extremal principle, congruence, symmetry, similarity or counting.

5

Prove

Convert intuition into a chain of justified statements. Handle every case and equality condition.

6

Polish

Remove unnecessary work and make the key idea easy for a reader to see.
