Core topic

Number Theory

Study the hidden structure of integers and learn why divisibility can be surprisingly powerful.

Olympiad Mathematics

Essential ideas

Focus on divisibility, greatest common divisors, prime factorization, modular arithmetic and Diophantine equations.

a ≡ b (mod n) ⇔ n | (a − b)

Useful habits

  • Factor expressions before expanding.
  • Check parity (odd/even) early.
  • Use the Euclidean algorithm for gcd questions.
  • Choose a modulus that simplifies the expression.
  • Look for prime divisors and contradiction arguments.

Mini example

If an integer n is divisible by both 6 and 15, then it is divisible by lcm(6,15)=30. Such small structural observations often unlock larger proofs.
